Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 12, 2026

Welcome to Guitar Tool, a mobile application developed by Travel Fall Tech. Your privacy is paramount to us. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with your use of our mobile app.

Summary: Guitar Tool is designed with privacy first. The app operates completely offline, processes all audio data locally on your device, and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information, usage statistics, or device identifiers. When you use the app, no data is sent back to Travel Fall Tech or any third-party servers.

2. Device Permissions & Audio Processing

To provide its core functionality, Guitar Tool requires access to your device's microphone:

  • Microphone Permission: Used solely to capture audio frequency for pitch detection. This allows the app to determine if your guitar strings are in tune.
  • Local Processing: All microphone input is processed in real-time, in memory, directly on your physical device.
  • No Recording or Storage: We do not record, store, save, or transmit any audio data. As soon as pitch calculations are completed, the audio data is discarded instantly.

3. Third-Party Services

Guitar Tool does not include any third-party advertising networks, analytics packages, or tracking SDKs. There is no integration with services that collect user profiles or track behavior across apps.

4. Children's Privacy

Because our application does not collect any personal information or track users, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) standards. It is safe for use by individuals of all ages.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top.
